# Liquidity dataset provenance

Retrieved 2026-08-31.

- `raw_stablecoincharts_all.json`: DefiLlama public endpoint
  `https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoincharts/all`, SHA-256
  `a151bd18b851be0ad4032eedd282e425da7bef9eee387a8722e4b8a55e29f1a1`.
- `liquidity_daily.csv`: generated by `build-liquidity-data`, SHA-256
  `5edc6a144c137f5b8b4e7e47904a15ad9ea37797725fba2ad2f1ef8c75ee093a`.
- BTC trend is derived from `binance_daily.csv` close prices without future data.
- BTC/ETH ETF flows, total-market trend, funding and open-interest fields are blank.
  Farside pages were browser-readable but returned HTTP 403 to archival download;
  complete historical Binance open interest was not available from the public API.

The resulting score has 40% configured component-weight coverage. It is suitable
only for a preliminary stablecoin-plus-BTC-trend ablation, not for evaluating the
full liquidity model.
